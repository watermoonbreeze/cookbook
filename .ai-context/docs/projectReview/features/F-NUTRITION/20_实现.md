## 代码落点
- 摄入折算[真相源](整份×eaten_ratio×成员share·三维正交守恒): S domain/model/IntakeCalculator
- 今日营养卡: A ui/home/HomeViewModel(todayNutrition)
- 今日餐食输入统一使用 `MealDayCardProjector` 产出的 `DayMealCardData`，营养聚合继续只读取卡片内容，不改变营养口径，也不向 Meal Projection 写入营养状态。
- 色系墙(膳食结构·不关联热量慢病): A ui/home/NutritionWall; S domain/NutritionLevel,NutritionInterpreter,NutrientLevelFilter
- 热量/营养值(resolveGrams): S domain/model/Nutrition, data/repository/NutritionRepository
- 周计划营养线(宝塔四层缺口): S domain/NutritionLine,NutritionLineAdvisor; A ui/component/NutritionLineCard
- 趋势折线/宏量条: A ui/component/NutritionTrendChart,NutritionMacro,DishNutritionLine
- 营养表: A ui/nutrition/NutritionTableScreen,NutritionTableViewModel
- 饮食报告: A ui/report/DietReportScreen,DietReportViewModel; S domain/model/DietReport
- 开关默认值: S domain/model/Preference(PreferenceKeys.DEFAULT_*)
