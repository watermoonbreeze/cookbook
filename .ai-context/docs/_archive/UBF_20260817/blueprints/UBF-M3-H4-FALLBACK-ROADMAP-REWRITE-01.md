# UBF M3 H4 Fallback — Roadmap Rewrite 01

## 1. Decision

The UBF roadmap no longer requires an empirically supported one-dimensional ladder as the sole route into Universal Contracts. It now has two mutually explicit paths:

- `M4-L` — Level-based Universal Contracts. Dormant while H4 persists. Entry requires accepted naturalistic evidence, authorized reanalysis and a supportable H1/H2 result.
- `M4-NO` — Non-Ordinal Universal Closure Contracts. Conditionally reachable from the accepted Decision Record candidate after M3 End/Accept and an explicit M3→M4-NO handoff.

Selecting M4-NO is a reversible architecture-policy decision under uncertainty, not evidence for H3 and not a claim that a Level core can never exist.

## 2. M3 exit rewrite

M3 may End/Accept on the M4-NO path only when all are true: H4 is explicitly preserved; G05/G06 passive observation remains a non-blocking sidecar; the non-ordinal candidate and Schema pass independent challenge 10/10; no Universal Level/Profile/Selector/routing conclusion is inferred; and the handoff names M4-NO exclusively.

The empirical lane is suspended from the critical path, not closed or satisfied. A future accepted evidence event may separately reopen analysis and propose switching paths; it cannot silently mutate an accepted M4-NO contract.

## 3. Revised M4-NO outputs

M4-NO must produce:

1. Blueprint first-principles definition;
2. canonical Decision Record and domain-extension contracts;
3. four closure-state contracts and Residual Decision Register;
4. fail-closed Delegation Decision procedure, not a scalar Selector;
5. conditional capability-evidence predicate without model ranking;
6. Q/STOP and structured feedback contracts;
7. FULL/LITE carriers that do not weaken records;
8. GC validation/promotion lifecycle without counts-as-Level;
9. Delegation Preservation and over-design gates;
10. migration/reversibility contract for any future M4-L path.

M4-NO may refine candidate semantics only through its own Preview/Start and independent challenge. It cannot introduce Level count/name/threshold/envelope or legacy mapping.

## 4. Revised M5-NO outputs

M5-NO separates CookBook Overlay from the non-ordinal Core. Legacy L7 remains historical. GC-01..48 map to Decision Record/domain/evidence roles, not Universal Levels. Project constraints add or strengthen individual records; no project floor, score, rank or aggregate state is allowed. Pointer migration and historical acceptance continuity remain required.

## 5. M6–M8

M6 freezes the selected path's canonical mutation blueprint and independent challenge. M7 mechanically executes only the frozen path. M8 independently reviews, accepts and hands off. Existing TURN, remote visibility, capability-ledger, exact scope, Preserve and rollback contracts remain authoritative.

## 6. Stage gates

This rewrite does not End M3 or start M4. Required sequence is: roadmap rewrite ACCEPT → M3 End/Accept + M3→M4-NO handoff ACCEPT → separate M4-NO Preview/Start → Work packages. M4-L stays dormant; M5–M8 and CookBook Phase 3B remain not started.
